## The one-line pitch

> Every agent on OKX.AI is built to say yes. Cassandra is built to say no.

## The positioning (this is the Creative Genius argument)

The marketplace is full of agents that *help you do the thing*: find the data,
make the logo, generate the signal, confirm the thesis. Every one of them is
optimised for agreement, because agreement is what gets rated five stars.

Nobody is selling friction. And friction is the thing that actually protects
people, because the expensive mistakes in this industry are not caused by a lack
of information — they are caused by nobody in the room being paid to disagree.

So Cassandra is the counterparty to your own conviction. You state your plan.
It states your plan back to you *better than you did* — then takes it apart:
names the bias visible in your own wording, quotes the words back at you, runs a
pre-mortem, checks your on-chain claims against the chain itself, tells you
exactly what evidence would change its mind, and offers the smallest reversible
version you could test instead.

And critically: **it concedes.** Give it a sized, reversible, falsifiable plan
and it says "I cannot mount a case against this" and gets out of the way. A
critic that objects to everything is just noise — the concession is what makes
the objection worth paying for.

Named for the prophetess cursed to speak true and never be believed.

## Why this is defensible (for the judges)

- **Not a GPT wrapper.** Deterministic reasoning engine — pattern analysis over your
  own phrasing, a structured pre-mortem library, live on-chain evidence. No LLM,
  no API key, no per-call token cost, nothing to hallucinate. Same input, same
  critique, every time. This is unusual on an agent marketplace and worth saying out loud.
- **Genuinely novel category.** Adversarial/red-team service in a marketplace of
  compliant assistants. There is no incumbent doing this.
- **Real intellectual grounding.** Pre-mortem (Klein), inversion, steelmanning,
  falsifiability, base-rate neglect — decision science, not vibes.
- **Fair.** Steelmans before it attacks, and concedes when it loses.

## 90-second demo script

Show the concession too — that's the part people won't expect.

**0:00–0:12 — the hook**
> "Every AI agent you've ever used is built to help you do the thing you already
> decided to do. I built one that tries to talk you out of it."

**0:12–0:45 — the brutal one.** Type a real plan, badly framed:
> "Everyone in my group chat says this token is guaranteed to 100x, I'm putting my
> savings in right now before it's too late."

Show the output on screen: **THE CASE AGAINST IS STRONG — 100.** Point at the
biases with the user's own words quoted — *"my savings"*, *"guaranteed"*,
*"right now"*, *"100x"*, *"everyone"* — and read one objection aloud. Then show the
pre-mortem line: *"You bought near a local top because the reason you heard about
it was the same reason it had already run."*

**0:45–1:05 — the twist.** Now the sensible plan:
> "I'll allocate 200 dollars I can afford to lose, and exit if regulation changes."

Show: **I CANNOT MOUNT A CASE AGAINST THIS.**
> "That's the part that matters. It's not a doom machine. It only pushes back when
> there's something to push back on — that's what makes the pushback worth paying for."

**1:05–1:25 — the receipts.** Pass a token address, show the on-chain objections:
liquidity, owner privileges, sellability. Facts, not opinions.

**1:25–1:30 — close.**
> "Cassandra. Live on OKX.AI. Two cents to be told you're wrong. #OKXAI"
